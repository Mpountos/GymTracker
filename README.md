A simple and effective Gym Progress Tracking mobile application.

Navigation:
 - Create your Workout Plans: Create multiple fitness plans tailored to your goals. Add specific exercises with target sets, reps, and rest periods.
 - Plan your Week: Quickly see which days you trained and which workouts you performed. Map out your upcoming training sessions to ensure you never miss a workout.
 - View your Progress: Monitor the total weight lifted over time to ensure progressive overload. Track your Personal Records for specific lifts (e.g., Squat, Bench Press) through intuitive line graphs.
 
Data Management:
 - Cloud Exercise Database: Browse and add exercises seamlessly from a cloud-based database powered by Firebase Firestore.
